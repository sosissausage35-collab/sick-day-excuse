# Candidate lines — "The Funniest Sick-Day Excuse"

# coworker would laugh at; 2) FRESH idea (no fever/dog/dead grandmother);
# 3) twist/funniest word near the end; 4) sounds like a real person texting;
# 5) no wasted words.
